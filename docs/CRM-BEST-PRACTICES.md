# CRM Best Practices Built Into Launchpad CRM

This CRM implements proven solo-founder & cohort-business practices without bloated enterprise complexity:

1. **6-Stage Linear Pipeline:**
   - `Cold / Identified` → `Warm Lead / Inbound` → `Offer / Pay Link Sent` → `Active Student (Paid)` → `Graduated / Alumni` → `Closed Lost / Nurture`.
2. **Dynamic Fit + Behavioral Lead Scoring (0–100):**
   - Combines static fit (decision maker, track value, high-intent source such as free-help askers or Scorecard leads) with behavioral signals (viewed deck, replied, offer sent) so Hot Leads (≥65) surface automatically in Today's Queue.
3. **Deal Rot Alerts (>7 Days Stale):**
   - Any lead sitting in `Warm Lead` or `Offer Sent` for 7+ days without a logged touchpoint is flagged `ROT` so ghosted deals get a crisp Day 5 / Day 9 breakup message before dying silently.
4. **Speed-to-Lead & Follow-Up Cadences:**
   - Every lead carries a `Next Follow-Up` date and 1-click interpolated outreach/follow-up templates (`{{name}}`, `{{goal}}`, `{{company}}`).
5. **Student Delivery + Alumni Continuity Checklists:**
   - Tracks 24h Pre-Flight Checklist sent, Live Sprint attendance, Project Shipped, Review Captured, Masterclass Bonus Granted, and 1-on-1 / B2B Team Upsell Pitched so graduating students turn into testimonials, upsells, and corporate referrals.
6. **Zero-Server Persistence + Cross-Device Sync:**
   - Instant browser `IndexedDB` persistence + `localStorage` fallback + **Private GitHub Gist Sync** so your laptop and phone stay synced at \$0 cost.
